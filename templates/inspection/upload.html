Classification Criteria

Normal (Pass)

Uniform surface reflectivity, absence of deep tool chatter marks, micro-scratches within acceptable RMS deviation limits.

Defective (Reject)

Deep localized gouges, pitting corrosion, corner chips, thermal hairline fractures, or excessive burr formations.
